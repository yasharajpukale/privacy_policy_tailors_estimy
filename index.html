Tailors Estimy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 6 November 2026

Digital measurement register Ads served by Google AdMob No data sold, ever

The short version

Tailors Estimy is a register app a tailor shop uses to record its own customers' orders, measurements and payments. The shop types that data in, and the shop owns it. We store it for the shop on Google Firebase so it can be seen on the shop's own phones.

The app shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. Google, and ad networks Google works with, may use device identifiers and information about how you use apps to choose and measure the ads. You can switch off personalised ads or delete the advertising ID on your phone at any time — section 3 explains exactly what that means and gives the controls.

We do not read your contacts, your SMS or call logs, your photos (except the ones you deliberately attach to a receipt), or your location. The app asks the operating system for one permission only: internet access.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Tailors Estimy is developed and published by Yashraj Pukale, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us", "our"). This policy describes how the Tailors Estimy Android application handles personal information.

It covers the app and the Google Firebase services it talks to. It does not cover services you reach through the app that are run by other companies — WhatsApp, your phone app, your UPI app, or Google Play itself — which have their own policies; see section 14.

Two different groups of people are described in this policy, and it is worth keeping them apart:

  • The shop (you, the account holder). A tailor shop owner or staff member who registers, signs in, and enters data. For your account, we act as the data controller.
  • Your customers. The people whose names, phone numbers and measurements a shop writes into a receipt. The shop is responsible for that information and for telling those customers it is being recorded. We hold it only as the shop's processor — on the shop's account, visible only to that shop's devices.

If you run a shop: recording a customer's name and phone number for your own order book is normally a personal-use / business-record purpose. Keep your customer records for that purpose, and honour a customer's request to remove their entry — you can delete any receipt in seconds (section 10).

2. Information we collect

Everything in the first three rows exists because you type it. Nothing is scraped off your device.

2.1 Account information

  • Name and email address when you register with an email address, or when you sign in with Google — in the Google case we receive the display name and email address attached to your Google account, plus a Google account identifier.
  • A Firebase Authentication unique user ID for your account, and which sign-in method you used (Google or email and password). Passwords are hashed and managed by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password as plain text, and no code of ours reads it.

2.2 Shop information you fill in

  • Shop name, owner name, shop address, shop mobile number.
  • Your UPI ID (used to draw the payment QR code on receipts).
  • Your usual GST percentage and your per-product price list.
  • Two optional tagline lines printed on receipts, and your chosen interface language.
  • The next receipt serial number, so your register numbering stays unbroken.

2.3 Register entries you create (about your customers)

  • Customer name and phone number.
  • Measurement date, promised delivery date, and the garments ordered with quantities, styles and free-text notes.
  • Body measurements per garment (chest, waist, shoulder, sleeve, length and so on) — the core of the app.
  • Billing figures: amount, GST percentage, advance paid, payment status (pending / advance / fully paid) and payment mode (cash or UPI).
  • Any blackboard sketch you draw with a finger on the receipt. It is saved as an image attached to that receipt.
  • Any reference photos you choose to attach — fabric, style, or a finished garment — picked from your camera or gallery through the system picker.

2.4 Technical and advertising information

  • A device claim identifier that this app generates itself, plus a "last seen" timestamp. It is a random string created by the app — not your IMEI, not your Android ID, not a phone number — and it exists only so the app can tell which of a shop's phones is the first one signed in. That phone alone is offered the Change Password screen. Signing out releases the claim; clearing app storage or uninstalling retires it.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad data handled by Google AdMob once ads are switched on. This is described in full in section 3, because it is Google's processing, not ours.
  • Error text shown to you on screen when a save or sign-in fails, so you know what went wrong. We do not run analytics, and we do not build behavioural profiles of you.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Tailors Estimy shows advertising. Ads in this app are delivered by Google AdMob, part of Google's advertising products. Depending on how our AdMob account is configured, ads may also be filled by other ad networks and ad technology partners that Google works with.

3.1 What this means in practice

We place ad spaces in the app. We do not choose which ad you see, we do not target ads to you, and we cannot see a profile of you built from ad data. The ad selection, delivery and measurement are done by Google and its partners on their own systems.

3.2 Information Google and its partners may collect through ads

  • Your device's advertising ID (the Google Advertising ID / AAID on Android). This is the identifier used to connect ad activity across apps on one phone.
  • Device and app information: model, make, operating system version, language, resolution, and the fact that this app is installed and running.
  • Ad interactions: which ad was shown, when, for how long, whether you tapped it, and whether you later installed or opened the advertised app.
  • Information from cookies or similar technologies on related Google services.
  • Approximate location that Google may derive from the above signals, as described in Google's own privacy policy. Our app does not ask your phone for GPS or location permission.

3.3 Why they collect it

To select and serve ads that are more relevant to you, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure the performance of ads for advertisers, and to prevent fraud.

3.4 Your advertising choices — these are on your phone, not buried in our app

  • See and control the topics and personalised ads Google shows you: My Ad Center (myadcenter.google.com) and Ads Settings.
  • Reset or delete the advertising ID entirely: on your phone, open Settings → Google → Ads and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. Deleting it stops the identifier being used for personalised advertising.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising across apps and websites: aboutads.info/choices and, in Europe, youronlinechoices.com.
  • Understand what Google does with this data: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's list of ad technology partners. The ad networks that may fill our ad spaces are listed by Google at AdMob ad technology partners.

3.5 Consent, in regions that require it

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising that uses identifiers or derives personal data from your activity requires your consent. In those regions Google's ad services ask you, through a consent message shown inside the app, whether you agree to personalised ads and to the use of a device advertising identifier. You can review or change that choice at any time in the app's settings area and in your device's ads settings. If you decline, you will still see ads — they are simply not personalised. Outside those regions, ads may be served on the basis of the legitimate interest described by Google above.

3.6 What we do not do with ads

We do not sell or rent your personal information to advertisers. We do not send your customer list, your measurements, your receipts, your shop details or your contacts to Google for advertising. Advertisers are shown aggregate counts — how many people saw or tapped an ad — never your data.

3.7 Ads and this app's audience

Tailors Estimy is a business tool for shop owners and staff. It is not directed at children, is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme, and is not configured as a child-directed or "Designed for Families" app. Ads served in it are for an adult audience.

4. How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and let you recover access when a password is forgotten.
  • To show your register: search a customer by name or phone, list receipts, filter by payment status, and total your dues and payouts.
  • To generate the receipt PDF, the printed measurements, the UPI QR code and the WhatsApp message text you send to a customer.
  • To decide which of a shop's signed-in phones is allowed to change the account password.
  • To display the app in the language the shop chose.
  • To show advertising through Google AdMob, as described in section 3, which is how the app is funded.
  • To respond when you contact us about a problem or a data request, and to meet legal and tax record-keeping obligations.

We do not use your information to train advertising models, to build a profile of your habits for sale, or to make automated decisions about you. There is no such decision-making in this app.

5. What we do not collect

To be concrete, because "we may collect information" clauses are unhelpful. Tailors Estimy does not read or upload:

  • Your phone's contacts / address book. Customer phone numbers only exist because someone typed them into a receipt.
  • Your SMS, call log or call content. Tapping the call button hands the number to your phone app; nothing comes back to us.
  • Your photo gallery. Photos are uploaded only when you choose one for a specific receipt, through the system picker.
  • Your precise or coarse location, GPS or cell-tower data. The app declares no location permission.
  • Your microphone, camera feed, files outside the app, clipboard history, browsing history, or app list.
  • Your health, financial-account, or government-identifier data. A UPI ID you enter for your own shop is the only payment identifier stored, and it is stored to print on receipts.
  • IMEI, Android ID, serial number or any hardware identifier.

The distinction that matters: our app does not collect the above, while Google AdMob does collect advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data under section 3. That is Google's processing, and your controls for it are in your device and Google ad settings.

6. Device permissions

Tailors Estimy declares a single Android permission of its own: INTERNET. It is needed to sign in, sync your register, generate cloud-backed receipts and load ads.

When you attach a photo, the operating system's own picker or camera app is opened on your behalf; the app does not hold a standing camera or storage permission for anything else. When you tap call, WhatsApp, or a UPI button, your phone hands the action to the app you chose — that app then has whatever data is in the link (a phone number, a payment amount).

8. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information reaches third parties in only these ways:

  • Google (Firebase / Cloud Firestore / Cloud Storage). Our hosting and authentication provider. It processes your data to run the service under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob and its ad technology partners. They receive the advertising and ad-interaction data listed in section 3 in order to serve and measure ads.
  • Your own shop's devices. Anyone signed into your account on any device sees your register. That is the point of a shared register — keep your password to yourself, and only hand out devices you control.
  • People you deliberately send something to. When you tap Share, the receipt PDF and message text leave the app through WhatsApp or the Android share sheet, and are then governed by that app's policy.
  • Where the law requires it. If we are served with a valid legal order, we may disclose the minimum required and tell you unless legally barred from doing so.
  • If the app or business is transferred. Should Tailors Estimy ever be sold or handed over, customer data would move with it, and we would notify you before that happened.

We do not receive information about you from third parties, other than the sign-in details Google provides when you choose Sign in with Google.

9. Where data is stored, and international transfers

Your account, register and photos are hosted on Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure. Data may be stored and processed on servers outside India, including outside your country, and Google's personnel may access it for the limited purposes described in their terms.

Where personal data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred abroad, Google's standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards apply to Firebase and to Google's ad products. The Google Play Store itself is operated by Google Ireland Limited for users outside the United States.

10. Retention and deletion

10.1 You can delete most of it yourself, immediately

  • A receipt can be deleted from the Generated Receipt screen. Deleting it removes the Firestore document and the photos attached to it from Cloud Storage. The removal is permanent; there is no trash folder.
  • Shop details, prices, GST rate and language can be edited or cleared at any time in Settings.
  • Signing out releases this phone's device claim.

10.2 Deleting your account

Write to [[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]] from the email address on the account and ask for account deletion. We will remove your Firebase Authentication user, your Firestore documents and your stored photos, normally within 30 days, and confirm when it is done. Deletion requests are free.

10.3 Backups and copies you made

Receipt PDFs already shared with a customer, or messages already in WhatsApp, are outside our control from the moment you send them. Google's ad data is retained under Google's own schedules, which you can shorten by deleting your advertising ID. We may keep minimal records that law requires, and anonymised counts that cannot be traced back to you.

11. Security

  • All traffic between the app and Firebase, and between the app and AdMob, travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Firestore and Storage security rules confine every read and write to the signed-in owner's own user branch, so one shop cannot address another shop's documents.
  • Passwords are hashed and verified by Firebase Authentication; they are never written into the database or a log.
  • The Change Password screen is reachable only from the shop's first signed-in device, which limits what a lost or shared phone can do to the account.

No internet service is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will contact you and describe what we know and what we are doing, as soon as we are able.

12. Children

Tailors Estimy is a business register for adults who run or work in a shop. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), we do not knowingly collect their data, and we do not enrol it in Google Play's Families programme or serve child-directed ads.

Measurements recorded in the app may include a child's clothing sizes as part of a family's tailoring orders. That entry is a business record held on the shop's own account, and the shop is responsible for it.

13. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — everything the app holds about your shop is visible and editable inside the app.
  • Erasure — delete receipts yourself, or ask us to delete the account.
  • Portability — you can generate PDFs of your receipts at any time.
  • Object and withdraw consent — including your advertising consent, through My Ad Center, Ads Settings, and your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Complain — to us first, and if you are not satisfied, to your local data protection authority, or to Google Play for policy matters.

To exercise any of these, email [[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]]. We may ask you to confirm the request from the address on the account. We answer within 30 days.

14. Other apps and services

The app can hand you off to software we do not control. Each one then works under its own policy, and the information in the hand-off goes with it:

  • WhatsApp — receives the receipt message text and PDF you chose to share, including the customer's number.
  • Your phone/dialler — receives the number you tapped to call.
  • UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others) — receive the payee ID and amount encoded in the QR or payment link.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play — see Google's privacy policy and Play terms.
